LUNG VISION AI

Clinical Pulmonary Screening Platform

🎯 Objective & Clinical Purpose

Lung Vision is an AI-powered thoracic triage platform designed to assist radiologists and healthcare providers in rapid, accurate detection of pulmonary pathologies from digital chest radiographs (CXR). It accelerates clinical workups and provides automated risk indexing for prioritized patient care.

🧠 Multi-Model AI Architecture

DenseNet121 Feature Reuse Architecture
ResNet50 Deep Residual Learning
EfficientNetB0 Scaled Compound Coefficients

⚡ Ensemble Consensus Voting is applied across models to determine champion inferences and minimize false positives.

🔬 Targeted Classes

Tuberculosis Pneumonia COVID-19 Lung Opacity Normal
🛡️ Quality Gatekeeper: Input scans undergo automated pre-validation ensuring correct thoracic PA projection before clinical inference.
